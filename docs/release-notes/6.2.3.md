## What's new

**See-through popups**
- The command palette, search (⌘R), the history and clipboard pickers, the shortcuts sheet (⌘/) and AI help now float over your work as a blurred, see-through overlay tinted with your terminal theme, instead of a solid window.

**Install with Homebrew**
- `brew install --cask nevin/tap/anser` installs Anser in one step. After that, Anser keeps itself up to date as before.
