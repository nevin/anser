## What's new

**A picture behind your terminal, or a see-through window**
- **Settings → Appearance → Background** now offers Solid colour, See-through or Image.
- Choose any picture and how it fits: Fill, Fit, Stretch or Center. The terminal's colour sits over it at the opacity you pick, so text stays readable.
- See-through shows what's behind the window. Colour opacity now goes down to 10%.
- The status row at the bottom stays solid in every mode.

## Fixes

- **A long command from history, or a big paste, no longer covers the output.** The input starts on the next row as usual. When the screen is full, the terminal makes room for the input instead of being drawn over.
- **Closing a tab that floods its title or output is instant again.** It could take from 5 seconds to over a minute.
