## What's new

**See what your tabs are running**
- The sidebar's **Servers** section is now **Running**. Under each tab it lists the commands and background jobs that tab started (`npm run dev`, `cmd &`, `nohup …`), with each one's CPU, memory and ports.
- Click a process to go to its tab, hover to stop it, or right-click to open its port, copy its command line, force quit it, or stop everything in that tab.
- **Left over from closed tabs** lists anything still running after its tab closed, with **Stop all**.
- **Other programs on ports** lists everything else of yours listening on a port (Docker, a database, a dev server from another app). Stopping one asks first. Ports used by macOS itself are hidden and never stopped.
- **All processes…** opens Activity Monitor.

**"Port already in use" tells you who has it**
- When a command fails because its port is taken, a red chip says what holds it, like **:3000 is taken by node**. Click it to stop that process and run your command again.

**A warning when something runs away**
- A background job that keeps a CPU core busy for two minutes, or anything holding over 4 GB of memory, gets an orange chip so you can stop it.
