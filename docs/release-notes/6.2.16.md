## Updated terminal engine

- Anser now runs on the latest Ghostty core. It brings faster text handling, prompts that no longer leave stale copies behind after a resize, rows that stay visible when a fallback font can't be loaded, and a batch of stability fixes.
- Copy on select stays off unless you turn it on in Settings (the new engine would otherwise turn it on by itself).

## Fixes

- **Results and times have room.** A failed command's `✗ exit 1` and its time no longer run into each other.
- **Cleaner top edge.** No faint line above the first command, right under the tab bar.
- **Quieter single tab.** With one tab open, its × shows when you point at the tab.
