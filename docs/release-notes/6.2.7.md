## What's new

**Shift+↑ / Shift+↓ scroll the terminal**
- Scroll a line at a time without reaching for the mouse, at the prompt or while a command runs.
- In a command of several lines, Shift+↑/↓ still selects text. Full-screen programs like vim and less get the keys as before.

**Many tabs use much less memory**
- Hidden tabs no longer keep their own copy of the screen in graphics memory. With 10 tabs open, Anser uses about half the memory it did (193 MB → 89 MB).
- A tab you haven't looked at for 10 seconds lets go of its last picture too. Tabs you flip between quickly still show instantly.

## Fixes

- **A program that sets its title over and over could slow Anser to a crawl.** Opening or closing other tabs could take minutes. Title changes are now handled one at a time per tab.
- **A detached shell printing nonstop could hold up every other tab.** The helper that keeps your shells running now takes turns between them, and stays fast with output that has no line breaks.
- Closing a tab that's busy printing always ends its shell.
